    The packet

    Every message is one radio packet: a 32-byte header, the encrypted name and text, and a 16-byte tag. Type below to see it rebuild. The bytes shown are placeholders in the real layout. On a board, the body is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

    OffsetSizeField

    Header bytes are readable by anyone listening. The nonce is sent in the clear. The ciphertext holds the name and text, and the tag proves nothing was altered.
